1. Send your request

Email tallyoralabs@gmail.com with the subject LUMENFOLD account deletion. If you linked an email, send from that address. Include your public player name and, if available, the player identifier from the game. For a guest account, provide the player name or identifier you still have; we will explain the available ownership checks.

2. Verify ownership

We reply to confirm which account is involved and verify ownership before deleting data. Do not send your password, sign-in codes, full card details or identity documents in the initial email. A request is not an automatic or instantaneous deletion.

3. What the request covers

Deletion covers the identified server account and associated email identity, profile and leaderboard entry, sessions, world/building progress, in-game resources and puzzle records. Removing an account permanently loses the associated progress. Ask explicitly if you want only a specific category reviewed rather than the entire account.

Retention and confirmation

Account deletion unlinks purchase records from the game account. Minimal records containing a purchase-token hash, product, Credits amount and delivery status/time remain to prevent repeat redemption; pending tokens remain encrypted until delivery confirmation is resolved. Google Play keeps its own transaction records independently. No fixed automatic purge interval for the minimal replay-prevention record or recovery backups is represented here. We explain any additional specific legal or security retention and backup handling in our response and confirm the outcome.

Local device data

Uninstalling LUMENFOLD or clearing its Android storage removes local saves and preferences but does not by itself delete the server account. To remove both, request account deletion and clear local app data. A guest account that was never linked may become harder to identify after local data is removed.
