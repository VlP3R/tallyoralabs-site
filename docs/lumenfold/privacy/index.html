Who is responsible

LUMENFOLD: Pocket Worlds is developed and published by Stylianos Minoglou under the name TallyoraLabs, Greece. Contact: tallyoralabs@gmail.com. This notice covers the current pre-release Android game and its developer-operated online game services.

Information used by the game

  • Account and session data: a guest/player identifier, installation or device identifier used for sessions, authentication tokens and related creation, expiry and security timestamps.
  • Optional email account: your email address, a protected password representation, verification/recovery records and email-delivery records when you link an email account. Passwords are not stored in plain text.
  • Game activity: puzzle runs and accepted results, world/building progress, in-game resources, upgrades, production and collection records.
  • Player profile: a generated or chosen username and ranking based on server-verified puzzle completions.
  • On-device data: saved sessions, tutorial and puzzle checkpoints, selected world targets and settings such as sound and reduced motion.
  • Support: your email and information you voluntarily send when requesting help.

How it is used

We use this information to operate the game, save and restore the same player’s progress, authenticate accounts, deliver verification or recovery email, validate rewards, prevent duplicate or abusive actions, provide the leaderboard and answer support requests. On-device preferences keep your selected settings. Network connections also reveal your IP address to the systems handling the connection.

What other players can see

The Top 100 and your ranking use the public username and verified puzzle total/rank. Your email, password and session credentials are not public profile information. Choose a username that does not disclose private information.

Sharing and service providers

We do not sell player data. The online game API and database are hosted on DigitalOcean in Frankfurt, Germany (EU), under the developer’s control. Game connections use HTTPS. Encrypted recovery backups are held separately under the developer’s control. Support email is handled through Google Gmail. These systems process information needed for their corresponding functions. Information may also be disclosed when legally required or necessary to address security abuse.

Google Play purchases

The internal Android test build includes Google Play Billing for optional consumable Credits packs. Checkout is currently limited to designated studio license testers; public purchases are not enabled. A linked email game account is required. Google handles checkout and payment details under its privacy policy; we do not receive payment-card details. The app sends Google a pseudonymous game-account identifier to bind the purchase to the correct player. It sends the purchase token and product identifier to our HTTPS server, which checks the purchase with Google before delivering Credits. We keep the product, Credits amount, delivery time, test status and token hash linked to the game account for delivery and prevention of repeat redemption.

Purchase records and deletion

Pending purchase tokens are separately encrypted while delivery confirmation is retried, then erased when confirmation succeeds. Account deletion removes the purchase record’s link to your game account. A minimal token-hash record remains to prevent repeat redemption; an encrypted pending token may remain until delivery processing is resolved. Deleting the game account does not erase records held independently by Google Play. We have not introduced a fixed automatic purge period for the minimal replay-prevention record.

Refunds and Credits

We check Google Play refund and cancellation information to correct the Credits balance. Refund status, affected Credits, recovered Credits and the processing time are recorded with the purchase. Repeated notifications do not cause repeated deductions. If refunded Credits have already been spent, the balance is not made negative; further checkout is paused for support review while gameplay remains available. This does not initiate a real-money repayment.

Advertising and external sign-in

The current build has no active advertising SDK, third-party analytics SDK or Google sign-in integration. Linking a game email account is separate from signing into Google Play for checkout. Future advertising or other data practices will be described here before introduction.

Storage, security and retention

Server-side account and game records are kept to support the player’s continuing account and progress. Session tokens and recovery codes have validity limits; expiring a token does not delete the player account. Saved sessions use protected storage where supported. We retain support correspondence as needed to handle the request. You may request account/data deletion as described below. Where specific records must be retained for a legitimate security or legal reason, we will explain the categories, reason and applicable retention period in the response.

Your choices and deletion

You can play with a guest identity, choose whether to link email and edit your public username. Guest play still uses a server account; uninstalling the game does not by itself delete that server account. To request deletion, use the LUMENFOLD account and data deletion page. You can also contact us to request access to or correction of your personal information. Requests are reviewed to verify ownership and protect other players’ data.

Children

LUMENFOLD is intended for teenagers aged 13 and above and adults; it is not designed specifically for younger children. We do not ask players for their birth date, precise location, contacts, microphone recordings or payment-card details in the reviewed build. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information can contact us for review and deletion. The store content rating is separate from the intended audience.

This website and changes

Visiting this website is separate from using the Android game. See the website privacy notice for hosting logs and email links. Changes to the game’s data practices will be reflected here with an updated date.

Legal grounds

Account and game data needed to provide the requested game service are processed to perform that service. Protecting accounts, validating rewards and preventing abuse serve our legitimate interests in a reliable, secure game, subject to players’ rights. Support correspondence is used to respond to the requested help. We process information to meet a legal obligation where one applies. Optional email linking is a choice; without it, email-based account recovery is unavailable.

Privacy rights and support email

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal data and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact tallyoralabs@gmail.com. You may complain to your local data protection authority, including the Hellenic Data Protection Authority in Greece. See the European Commission’s information on individual rights. We use Gmail (Google) for support correspondence; its service infrastructure may process data internationally under the safeguards described in Google’s privacy policy. We use support messages for responding to your request and protecting the correspondence, not for unsolicited marketing.
