Who runs this website

This is the TallyoraLabs website for LUMENFOLD: Pocket Worlds and Wonderleaf, published by Stylianos Minoglou, Greece. Contact: tallyoralabs@gmail.com.

Hosting and technical data

The website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors’ IP addresses for security purposes, even if they are not signed into GitHub. See GitHub’s privacy statement for its processing and retention practices.

No advertising or visitor analytics

This site includes no advertising, analytics, tracking pixels, third-party fonts or embedded social-media widgets. The site itself sets no cookies and stores no visitor preferences. Language switches are ordinary links between English and Greek pages.

Contact and deletion requests

Email links open your email application. No message is sent automatically and no details are submitted to this website. If you email us, we receive your address and the information you send, use it to handle the request and keep correspondence as needed for that purpose. Requests about children should be sent by a parent or guardian without unnecessary identifying information.

Your choices

You can read all pages without an account. Contact us to ask about, correct or delete information you have sent by email. For app-specific data, use the separate privacy and deletion pages for LUMENFOLD or Wonderleaf.

Privacy rights and support email

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal data and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact tallyoralabs@gmail.com. You may complain to your local data protection authority, including the Hellenic Data Protection Authority in Greece. See the European Commission’s information on individual rights. We use Gmail (Google) for support correspondence; its service infrastructure may process data internationally under the safeguards described in Google’s privacy policy. We use support messages for responding to your request and protecting the correspondence, not for unsolicited marketing.
