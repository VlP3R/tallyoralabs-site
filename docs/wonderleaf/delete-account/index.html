Clear data on the device

An adult can open Android Settings → Apps → Wonderleaf → Storage (or Storage & cache) → Clear storage / Clear data. The wording varies by device. This resets reading progress, favorites, language and other app preferences. Uninstalling the app also removes its local app data; cloud backup is disabled for Wonderleaf.

No server account to delete

There is no server-side Wonderleaf reading account or child profile in the current version. We cannot remotely access or erase the local reading data on your device.

Delete support correspondence

A parent or guardian can email tallyoralabs@gmail.com with the subject Wonderleaf data deletion to ask us to delete information previously sent to support. Send from the address used for that correspondence. Do not include a child’s name or other identifying details. We verify the request and explain any specific legal retention obligation before confirming the outcome.
