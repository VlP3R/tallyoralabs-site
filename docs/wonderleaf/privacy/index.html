This notice describes the current pre-release Android version. We update it before introducing new data practices.

Who is responsible

Wonderleaf is developed and published by Stylianos Minoglou under the name TallyoraLabs, Greece. Parents and guardians can contact tallyoralabs@gmail.com. This notice covers the current offline pre-release Android storybook, designed for children around ages 5–8 and adults reading with them.

No account or online tracking in the Android app

The current Android app has no account creation, advertising, analytics, purchases or remote runtime assets. It requests no Internet, microphone or storage permission. Its stories, illustrations and sound effects are packaged with the app. The developer does not receive reading activity, favorites, children’s names, recordings or device identifiers from this build.

Information stored only on the device

Wonderleaf saves the selected language, reading position, favorites, completed-story state and preferences such as sound, larger text and reduced motion in local app storage. This information is used to resume the book and preserve your choices. It is not sent to a developer server. Android cloud backup for this app is disabled.

Narration and permissions

Narration is currently disabled. The retained narration implementation uses installed offline Android voices; it does not automatically download voices or send story text to cloud text-to-speech. Interactive illustration sounds remain local. The app does not record a child’s voice.

Parents and support

The parent area provides access to settings; its reading task is an interface barrier, not verified parental consent or account authentication. If an adult emails support, we receive their email address and whatever information they choose to include. Please do not send a child’s name, photograph, voice recording or other identifying information. Support correspondence is handled only to respond to the request and related follow-up.

Sharing and retention

The app does not transmit local reading data for us to share, sell or retain. Local progress and settings remain until app storage is cleared or the app is uninstalled. Support email is processed by the email service used to receive and reply to it; it is retained as needed for the support request. You can ask us to delete support correspondence, subject to any specific legal obligation that we explain in our response.

Deleting local data

There is no Wonderleaf server account to delete in this version. An adult can clear Wonderleaf’s storage in Android settings or uninstall the app to remove local progress, favorites and preferences. See Wonderleaf data deletion instructions.

Website and future changes

This public website has its own website privacy notice. It is not loaded by the Android storybook. Future purchases, narration changes or online features will be described in an updated policy before a corresponding version is distributed.

Privacy rights and support email

Where applicable, you can request access, correction, erasure, restriction or portability of personal data and object to processing based on legitimate interests. Contact tallyoralabs@gmail.com. You may complain to your local data protection authority, including the Hellenic Data Protection Authority in Greece. See the European Commission’s information on individual rights. We use Gmail (Google) for support correspondence; its service infrastructure may process data internationally under the safeguards described in Google’s privacy policy. We use support messages for responding to your request and protecting the correspondence, not for unsolicited marketing.
